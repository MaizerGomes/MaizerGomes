About

      
I am the Chief Technology Officer of Thembani Digital Financial Services, a digital-first microbank in Mozambique. I own the technology agenda: strategy, the teams and partners who deliver it, and the security, resilience and regulatory compliance a bank must have, so that more people can save, borrow and pay.

      
I am also General Director of Thembani Technology Solutions and founder of eKutiva Solutions (2016). Before banking I headed the academic software department at Universidade Pedagógica de Maputo, running national systems used by every campus. I came up through engineering, which keeps my decisions grounded, and I focus on turning technology into services that people in Mozambique can actually use.

Thembani Digital Banking Platform

USSD Banking & Mobile Money

            
Led the *778# banking channel and the mobile money integrations, so customers without a smartphone or data can check balances, top up, transfer, pay bills and request loans. One service works across three operators.

            
  • Inclusion

Interbank Payments Integration

            
Led the integration and coordinated user acceptance testing for RTGS and ACH payments with Banco de Moçambique and participating institutions, including the ISO 20022 messages, rejections and returns.

            
  • Regulator
  • RTGS · ACH
  • ISO 20022

          

          

            
Fraud & AML Monitoring

            
A monitoring service that scores customer and agent activity against 56 rules and screens names against sanctions and politically-exposed-person lists. Every rule starts in shadow mode and is tied to its legal basis, so compliance can trace why a decision was made.

            
  • Compliance
  • Risk
  • AML

          

          

            
Field Force Platform

            
Field operations for a mobile-money agent network. Promoters follow daily plans, visit agents, check in by location and onboard customers, even offline. Supervisors review every record and admins follow targets on one dashboard.
A result counts only after the operator confirms it, so reports and incentives rest on reconciled numbers.

            
  • Operations
  • Offline-first
  • Reconciliation

          

          

            
Microcredit Lending Platform

            
A platform for microfinance institutions and community lenders that automates the whole loan cycle: registration, disbursement, repayments through payment channels, and real-time compliance reports. Scoring and fraud checks run on machine learning hosted in-house.

            
  • Lending
  • Microfinance
  • AI scoring

          

          

            
KYC Face & Document Service

            
Remote customer verification: face matching, data extraction from Mozambican identity documents and cross-checks, so accounts can be opened digitally with a secure trail.

            
  • Onboarding
  • Identity

          

          

            
T-Mali Credit Engine

            
A credit decision service that turns customers’ financial behaviour into a score and a loan tier, from Micro to Platinum, with scheduled score decay so decisions follow recent behaviour.

            
  • Credit risk
  • Scoring

  • Bilingual

A “super app” for mothers who balance family, work and business. Delivered end to end for a client: iOS and Android apps, back end, admin dashboard, AI services and a bilingual landing page.
